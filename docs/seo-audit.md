# Search visibility audit — 17 October 2026

## Scope and audience

- Primary goal: organic traffic.
- Audience: businesses in Pakistan and internationally. The current site is English only, so no `hreflang` alternatives are warranted.
- Canonical domain: `https://monotone.digital`, confirmed by the site owner.
- Business claims and contact details come from the existing site content. No physical address or local business listing was invented.

## Changes implemented

- Every indexable page now has its own canonical URL, search title, description, and matching social metadata. Previously several pages inherited the homepage canonical.
- Organization, WebSite, and service structured data identify the business and its four services. Article and case-study data remain tied to the visible page content.
- The sitemap includes canonical, indexable pages. Article `lastmod` reflects a real content date; other pages no longer claim to have changed on every build.
- Placeholder legal pages are marked `noindex` and removed from the sitemap until approved copy exists.
- The process page now explains the four stages and links to services, packages, case studies, and contact.
- The homepage and a Pakistan pricing guide no longer promise AI search visibility or make unsupported absolute claims about indexing and low-cost sites.

## Content priorities after Search Console data arrives

1. Compare queries, pages, and countries over the last 3–6 months. Identify high-impression queries with weak click-through rates and relevant queries ranking near page one.
2. Map each query group to one existing destination: service, package, case study, or guide. Improve the matching page before creating a new one.
3. Strengthen the four service pages with specific project evidence and buyer questions where first-hand details are available. Add case-study proof only after the underlying figures are confirmed.
4. Review the Pakistan website-cost article against current quotes and update its examples as the market changes. Do not create city pages without genuine location-specific information.

## Launch and measurement

- Confirm the production site resolves, redirects alternate hosts to the canonical domain, and serves the generated `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`.
- Submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then inspect the homepage, service pages, case studies, and key guides for indexing and rendered HTML.
- Record a baseline of clicks, impressions, average position, indexed pages, and organic enquiries. Review by country and landing page monthly.
- Check Core Web Vitals with field data after launch; build-time checks cannot substitute for real-user performance.
- Replace placeholder legal copy and package guarantee conditions with approved text.

Search and AI answer inclusion cannot be guaranteed. Google and Bing both treat crawlability, accurate content, clear structure, and verifiable claims as the foundation for search and AI visibility; no special AI text file is required. See [Google's AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Google's generative AI guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), and [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a).
